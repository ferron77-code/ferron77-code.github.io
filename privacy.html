Jarvis privacy policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Jarvis is a private personal assistant that runs on its owner's own computer and is used only by its owner.

What Google data Jarvis uses

  • Google Calendar (calendar.events): to read my events, add events, and move events when I ask.
  • Gmail (gmail.readonly, gmail.compose): to read my mail when I ask about it, and to create drafts. Jarvis has no ability to send email; I review and send drafts myself.

How it is used

  • Data is read only to answer my own questions, at the moment I ask them.
  • To produce an answer, the question and the relevant calendar or mail content are sent to the AI model provider I have chosen (Anthropic, or OpenRouter when I switch models). Nothing else receives it.
  • Google data is not sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • Jarvis's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage

The Google sign-in token is stored only on my computer, in a file readable only by my user account. Mail and calendar content is not stored by Jarvis beyond the conversation in which I asked about it.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
